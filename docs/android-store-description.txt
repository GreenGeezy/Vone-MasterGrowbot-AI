MasterGrowbot AI is a cannabis grow assistant for home growers. Take a photo of your plant, get an AI-assisted plant health report with clear next checks, and follow your plant's progress in a grow journal.

AI PLANT HEALTH CHECK
• Take or upload a photo of your cannabis plant.
• Get a health score, what the AI noticed, and suggested next checks.
• Reports take your strain and your indoor, outdoor or greenhouse setup into account.
• Useful when you notice yellow leaves, spots, drooping, or signs of a possible nutrient deficiency, pests or mold.

7-DAY GROW PLAN
• Follow one plant with five quick photo check-ins over a week.
• See each new health score next to your last check-in.
• Finish the week with a day 1 and latest comparison.

GROW JOURNAL AND GROW TRACKER
• Save reports, photos and notes for each plant.
• Add grow tasks and follow-ups so nothing gets missed.
• Share a report card when you want a second opinion.

CANNABIS STRAIN GUIDE
• Search and browse 300 strain profiles.
• Add your own custom strains.

FOR NEW AND EXPERIENCED GROWERS
Whether it is your first grow or your tenth, MasterGrowbot AI helps you keep a clear record of your marijuana plants from seedling to harvest.
